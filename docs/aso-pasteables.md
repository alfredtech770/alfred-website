## Universal Links — verification

Once the new provisioning profile lands and the app ships to
TestFlight with Associated Domains enabled:

1. Open Settings → Developer → Universal Links Diagnostics on a
   device with the app installed.
2. Enter `https://alfredconcierge.app/catalog/dining` and confirm
   the system reports "App is associated".
3. Verify the AASA fetch in Console.app filtered by `swcd` — should
   see a successful fetch from `alfredconcierge.app`.

The web → app flow then works automatically: any iPhone Safari click
on an alfredconcierge.app link opens the app instead of the browser.

## What only you can do (not in this repo)

These can't be scripted from a codebase — they're App Store Connect
or developer-portal actions:

1. Paste the strings above into App Store Connect and submit for
   review. Apple typically clears metadata-only changes in 24 hours.
2. In Xcode → Signing & Capabilities → "+ Capability" → add
   **Associated Domains**. Xcode pushes the change to Apple's
   developer portal and re-issues the provisioning profile.
3. Commission or design the 10 screenshots + the 18-sec preview
   video using the briefs above.
4. Start the brand-defense Apple Search Ads campaign at $2/day.
5. Reply to every existing App Store review (open ones too). Apple
   weights "developer response present".
